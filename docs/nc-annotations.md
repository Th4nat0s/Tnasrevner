# NC annotations

`NC` is a reserved assignment meaning “intentionally not connected”. Activate
the **NC** button in the Tools palette, then Shift-click a board pad or schematic
pin. The assignment remains visible in the Connections and NET tables and is
saved with the project.

NC terminals are deliberately excluded from schematic electrical grouping and
wiring. Their visual marker indicates that the terminal was intentionally
processed, not electrically connected. To restore normal electrical behavior,
use the regular net assignment action and choose an ordinary net or clear the
assignment.
